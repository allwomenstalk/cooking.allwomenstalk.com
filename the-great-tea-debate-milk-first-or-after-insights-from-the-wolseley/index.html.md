# Tea Time Traditions: What The Wolseley Says

The age-old debate of whether to add milk before or after pouring tea continues to intrigue tea enthusiasts worldwide. The Wolseley, a renowned establishment celebrated for its exquisite dining experiences, dives into this discussion with insights that blend culinary science and tradition.

## A Matter of Taste

In the refined setting of The Wolseley, as depicted in their Instagram post (@thewolseley), the practice of adding milk significantly alters the flavor profile of tea. Pouring hot tea over milk ensures that the fats in the milk don't overheat, creating a milder, caramelized flavor. Meanwhile, adding milk after allows for better control over the tea's strength, offering a less dominant milky taste.

## Tradition Meets Taste

The Wolseley stands by the approach of adding milk after the tea is poured, highlighting the ease of adjusting the milk quantity to complement the desired tea strength. This approach reflects a thoughtful balance between adhering to traditional methods and catering to modern taste preferences.

## The Science Behind the Brew

The debate over milk's role in tea isn't solely about taste - it's a matter of science. Adding milk first or last significantly impacts the chemical reactions that occur within the cup. The initial milk addition prevents certain tannins in the tea from developing, altering the drink's astringency.
