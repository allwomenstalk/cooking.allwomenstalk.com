# 8 Fun April Fools' Day Recipes for Dinners, Desserts & Snacks

April Fool's Day Recipes are what you need to make the first of April memorable for your family. If you are searching for creative **april fools food ideas** or planning fun **april fools' day dinners**, you can do pranks of messy proportions—but if you don't like cleaning up, these **April Fool's Day recipes** are for you. Basically, the idea is to cook a dish that actually looks like something else entirely. From clever **april fools breakfast ideas** to crowd-pleasing **april fools party food** and sweet **april fools day desserts**, these edible tricks will surprise everyone at your table. Happy cookin' and foolin'!

## 1 Corn-on-the-Cob Cupcakes

![Corn-on-the-Cob Cupcakes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/11_360x274.jpg)

These look wonderfully made, don't they? If you are looking for festive **april fools cupcake ideas**, I love corn-on-the-cob, but I will not be disappointed when I bite into these and find out I'm eating a sweet cupcake instead. With yellow jelly beans arranged like corn kernels, these make fantastic **april snack ideas** that will be a massive hit with your kids.

**Quick Tips for Perfect Food Pranks:**

- Use familiar colors to sell the visual trick.
- Keep sweet and savory flavors clearly separated.
- Chill frosted treats so toppings hold their shape.

[Recipe](https://www.marthastewart.com/319042/corn-on-the-cob-cupcake?backto=true&backtourl=/photogallery/decorating-ideas-for-cupcakes)

## 2 Fakin' and Eggs

![Fakin' and Eggs](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/21_420x352.jpg)

What kid will refuse to eat sweets for breakfast? For a weird-looking treat and one of the easiest **april fools breakfast** options, this is a **great April Fool's Day recipe** you can whip up in minutes. The eggs are made of melted white chocolate and yellow M&Ms. The bacon strips are crafted from caramel squares and Tootsie Rolls. If you prefer a sweet trick after serving [savory meatloaf cupcakes](#4) for dinner, this morning surprise is a delightful way to start the day. Fun, right?

[Recipes](https://goo.gl/1RnktZ)

## 3 Cheddar Chive and Bacon Cupfakes with Avocado Frosting

![Cheddar Chive and Bacon Cupfakes with Avocado Frosting](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/31-560x420_560x420.jpg)

These look like they'll be sickly sweet, right? Well, they're not. With cheddar, bacon, chives, and avocado as main ingredients, these sure are strange-tasting cupcakes. But as soon as your mind gets over the notion that you should be eating dessert, you will find this **April Fool's Day dish** savory and satisfying for a unique **april fools day meal**.

[Recipe](http://willowbirdbaking.com/2009/07/11/cheddar-chive-and-bacon-cupfakes-with-avocado-frosting/)

## 5 Silly Cupcakes

![Silly Cupcakes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/5-_430x287.jpg)

Would you eat cupcakes that look like a bowl of spaghetti? Adventurous kids will probably gobble up these fun **April Fool's Day treats**. Similar to the trick used for [sweet corn-on-the-cob cupcakes](#1), these noodle illusions play with visual expectations. You don't really have to convince suspicious adults that these are actually edible, meaning more cupcakes for yourself!

![Compost Cake](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/61_420x341.jpg)

Cake that looks like dirt? Eww, right? If you want an unexpected **april fool cake** that double-takes as **april fools party ideas**, this compost cake is legendary. I can just imagine Calvin bringing this to school and eating it with gusto right in front of poor Susie Derkins. Ha!

## 7 Mini Burgers

![Mini Burgers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/71-560x372_560x372.jpg)

These look delicious! They will delight your family as sweet **april fools day snacks** because they are actually made of vanilla wafers, frosting, and Keebler Grasshopper cookies. I love the part where you dab the top with corn syrup and add sesame seeds to make the "burgers" look even more convincing. Pair these with [sweet sushi rolls](#8) for a full platter of dessert illusions.

**Fun Garnish Ideas for Dessert Sliders:**

- Green coconut flakes for lettuce
- Red frosting for ketchup
- Yellow Starburst squares for cheese slices

## 8 Sweet Sushi

![Sweet Sushi](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cooking/2012/03/8-_420x346.jpg)

Among these **April Fool's Day recipes**, this is most likely the one that would fool me best. I love California Maki, so I'd be pleasantly surprised to learn that all I'm eating is delicious pound cake topped with coconut flakes and fruit roll-ups. If you're in the mood for fooling someone who loves Japanese food, this is the ultimate **april fools food** recipe for your menu.

[Recipe](http://nicnacnoodle.blogspot.com/2011/04/april-fools-day-sweet-sushi-recipe.html)
